## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an executive personal portfolio experience grounded in prestige, restraint, and decisive authority. Taking inspiration from the historic, high-intensity heritage of Manchester United, the aesthetic marries athletic command with modern corporate luxury. It rejects decorative clutter in favor of stark architectural contrast, deep pitch-black depth planes, razor-sharp structural boundaries, and cinematic crimson radial light.

### Aesthetic Pillars
- **Executive Restraint & Minimalist Luxury:** Generous negative space, disciplined structural alignment, and whisper-quiet UI framing designed to showcase high-impact career milestones, leadership depth, and thought leadership.
- **Dynamic Heritage Contrast:** Pitch-black canvases anchored by surgical borders, punctuated by intense Red Devil crimson and deep Burgundy undertones, illuminated by rare, precise strikes of archival gold.
- **Architectural Depth:** Atmosphere created through dark-field layering, ultra-subtle red vignetting, and sharp hairline boundaries rather than muddy, generic box shadows.

### Emotional Response
The experience communicates unyielding confidence, seasoned strategic execution, institutional credibility, and bespoke craft. Visitors feel they are entering an elite executive briefing room or private digital gallery.

## Colors

The palette operates within strict tonal hierarchies to maintain an ultra-premium executive atmosphere. Crimson is wielded deliberately as a tool of primary hierarchy and momentum, rather than generic background wash.

### Color Tokens & Roles
- **Primary Red (`#DA291C`):** High-priority calls to action, primary metric emphases, active indicators, and critical timeline markers.
- **Deep Burgundy (`#8B0000`):** Used primarily in atmospheric ambient vignettes (`rgba(139, 0, 0, 0.28)`), gradient underlays, and active focus boundaries.
- **Gold Accent (`#FBE122`):** Reserved exclusively for prestige milestones, honors, board appointments, and verification emblems. Never used for large body surfaces.
- **Pitch Black (`#101010`):** Root canvas background. Provides true-black baseline contrast.
- **Surface Charcoal (`#1C1C1C`):** Primary structural surface for elevated cards, modal panels, and metric containers.
- **Secondary Surface (`#161616`):** Recessed containers, table strips, nested tags, and segmented bar backings.
- **Border Hairline (`#353535`):** Razor-sharp bounding borders on cards, table dividers, and structural containers.
- **Text White (`#FFFFFF`):** High-contrast display headlines, active navigation labels, and primary numeric readouts.
- **Secondary Text (`#BDBDBD`):** Body paragraphs, contextual metadata, subheadings, and secondary labels.
- **Tertiary/Muted Text (`#707070`):** Footnotes, timestamps, inactive states, and structural guidelines.

## Typography

The typographic pairing harnesses **Plus Jakarta Sans** for commanding, high-impact headings and metric callouts, paired with **Inter** for meticulous, clean, and uncompromised legibility across body narrative and dense data points.

### Typographic Principles
- **Tracking Discipline:** Larger headline tiers (`display-hero`, `headline-xl`) employ tight, decisive negative letter spacing to project weight and architectural firmness. In contrast, uppercase badges and category indicators (`label-badge`) apply deliberate positive letter spacing (`0.08em`) to convey editorial curation.
- **Numeric Clarity:** Metric figures utilize tabular lining figures where applicable, ensuring numerical alignment remains locked across the executive metric grids.
- **Hierarchy Separation:** Body paragraphs are constrained to neutral gray tones (`#BDBDBD`), safeguarding visual primacy exclusively for display titles and primary crimson highlights.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is constructed around an executive 12-column responsive grid on a maximum width container of `1280px`, anchored by a strict 4px/8px rhythm.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12 columns, `1.5rem` (24px) gutters, and `3rem` (48px) minimum margins.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8 columns, `1.25rem` (20px) gutters, and `2rem` (32px) margins.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, `1rem` (16px) gutters, and `1.25rem` (20px) outer margins.

### Spatial Rhythm
- **Macro Separation (`space-3xl`):** Major thematic sections (Hero, Credentials, Timeline, Publications, Contact) maintain generous `6rem` vertical margins to preserve breathing room and gravitas.
- **Component Breathing Room (`space-xl`, `space-lg`):** Cards, executive panels, and grid items employ internal padding of `1.5rem` to `2.5rem`, preventing density from feeling claustrophobic.
- **Micro Spacing (`space-xs`, `space-sm`):** Precise relationships between badge labels, metric numerals, and captions are maintained strictly at 4px and 8px intervals.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is conveyed through tonal field stratification, low-contrast structural outlines, and a distinct crimson atmospheric vignette, altogether avoiding generic grey drop shadows.

### Atmospheric Vignettes & Layering
1. **Base Floor (Z0 - Pitch Black):** The `#101010` viewport floor is infused with fixed, positioned radial glows (`radial-gradient(circle at 50% 20%, rgba(139, 0, 0, 0.28) 0%, rgba(16, 16, 16, 0) 65%)`). This produces a deep theatrical presence without introducing visual clutter.
2. **Elevated Surfaces (Z1 - Surface Charcoal):** Interactive cards, the navigation dock, and summary modules occupy `#1C1C1C`. They rely on crisp `1px solid #353535` boundaries to define physical limits against the pitch-black backdrop.
3. **Interactive & Highlight Elevation (Z2):** Hovering over cards lifts them with a targeted burgundy edge reflection (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 1px #DA291C, 0 8px 30px rgba(218, 41, 28, 0.12)`).
4. **Floating Overlays & Menus (Z3):** The persistent header and floating dropdowns use `#1C1C1C` with backdrop-filter blur (`16px`) and an alpha layer (`rgba(28, 28, 28, 0.85)`), edged by an ultra-thin hairline border (`#353535`).

## Shapes

The geometric form language adopts **Level 1 (Soft)** roundedness. It balances the modern precision of engineering discipline with tailored luxury. Sharp, rectangular authority dominates the silhouette, tempered only by subtle, restrained edge radii that eliminate harsh digital points.

### Radius Rules
- **Structural Cards, Panels, & Form Fields:** `0.375rem` to `0.5rem` (`rounded-md` / `rounded-lg`). Provides disciplined architectural framing.
- **Interactive Action Buttons & Inputs:** `0.25rem` (`rounded-sm`) to `0.375rem` (`rounded-md`). Keeps CTA elements tailored and chiseled.
- **Status Pills, Tags, & Metric Badges:** Complete capsules (`9999px`) are reserved solely for metadata chips and achievement pills, differentiating categorical tags from actionable structural components.

## Components

### Header & Navigation
- **Architecture:** Fixed or sticky frosted bar (`rgba(28, 28, 28, 0.85)`, backdrop blur `16px`, bottom border `1px solid #353535`).
- **Identity:** Monogram or bespoke lockup in `#FFFFFF` with primary red period mark (`.`).
- **Explore Dropdown:** Minimalist chevron toggle opening a panel styled in `#1C1C1C`, partitioned with subtle borders, categorizing career chapters, board engagements, and keynote materials.
- **CV / Resume CTA:** Border button in `#DA291C` with crisp text and red glow transition on hover.

### Hero Section
- **Composition:** Centered executive title lockup framed over the muted red radial vignette.
- **Structure:** Monospaced or tracked uppercase badge (`label-badge`) at the crown: `EXECUTIVE BRIEF // STRATEGIC LEADERSHIP`, followed by high-contrast headline (`display-hero`), contextual summary paragraph (`body-lg`), and dual CTAs (Primary Red Fill + Ghost Outline).

### Highlight Metric & Credential Cards (6-Card Grid)
- **Structure:** 3-column desktop / 2-column tablet / 1-column mobile grid.
- **Card Surface:** Background `#1C1C1C`, border `1px solid #353535`.
- **Content Flow:** Upper corner contains tiny categorized index (`01`, `02`) or mini icon; center showcases massive statistic (`metric-display`) in `#FFFFFF` or `#DA291C`; base contains title (`headline-sm`) and narrative caption (`body-sm`, `#BDBDBD`).
- **State:** On cursor hover, border transitions from `#353535` to `rgba(218, 41, 28, 0.6)`.

### Interactive Timeline
- **Spine:** 2px vertical guide rail rendered in `#353535`.
- **Node Indicators:** `#101010` core encircled by `#DA291C` active halo. Key milestone nodes feature a gold center spark (`#FBE122`).
- **Content Blocks:** Alternating or aligned `#161616` cards containing tenure period badges (`label-badge`), executive role title, company entity, and quantifiable achievements.

### Publication & Thought Leadership Cards
- **Visuals:** Horizontal or vertical format with dark, high-contrast imagery bordered by `#353535`.
- **Content:** Outlet name and date pill, bold article title in `#FFFFFF`, and an external link arrow icon that shifts `#DA291C` on hover.

### Gold-Accented Achievement Cards
- **Prestige Delineation:** Distinct from standard metrics, these celebrate awards, institutional honors, or board seats.
- **Accenting:** Features an archival gold top-border accent (`2px solid #FBE122`), gold icon insignia, and metadata badge with subtle gold tinting (`rgba(251, 225, 34, 0.1)` fill, `#FBE122` text).

### Categorized Skill & Competency Tags
- **Surface:** `#161616` background, border `1px solid #353535`, pill shape.
- **Typography:** `label-badge` in `#BDBDBD`.
- **Grouping:** Categorized headers (e.g., *Governance*, *Scale*, *Technology*) in primary red or muted caps.

### Refined Contact Form & Executive Footer
- **Form Inputs:** Surface `#161616`, border `1px solid #353535`, focus state `1px solid #DA291C` with soft red outer ring. Placeholder text `#707070`, input text `#FFFFFF`.
- **Submit Action:** Full-width or auto-width solid `#DA291C` button, black/white high-contrast text.
- **Footer:** Deep pitch-black background, legal and social directory links with muted transition states, closing with an understated Manchester United-inspired red architectural underline bar.
